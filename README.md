# 🍽️ GomaRestro - Restaurant & Lounge System

**The Simple, Fast POS Built for Restaurants, Lounges & Bars in Uganda.**

No internet needed. No monthly fees. Works on any Windows PC.

## ⬇️ Download - For Restaurant Owners

**Don't know coding? Just download and double-click:**

1. Go to [**Releases**](https://github.com/YOURNAME/GomaRestro/releases)
2. Download `GomaRestro.exe` from the latest version
3. Double-click it. Done. No installation needed.

> Your data is saved in `C:\Users\YourName\GomaRestro\` - Safe even when you update to a new version.

---

## ✨ What It Can Do - V1.0.0

- 🪑 **Tables View** - See all your tables at a glance. Free / Occupied
- ⚙️ **Full Settings** - Change Business Name, Phone, Location, TIN, VAT %, Service Charge, Receipt Footer
- 💾 **Auto Backup** - Every time you close the app, it backs up automatically. Keeps last 30 backups.
- 💾 **Backup to Flash** - One click to copy backup to flash drive
- 📋 **Activity Log** - See who changed what and when. Staff accountability.
- 👤 **User Roles** - Admin, Waiter, Cashier with PIN login
